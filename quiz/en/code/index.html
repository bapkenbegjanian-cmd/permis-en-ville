    0.2 g per litre of blood · 0.5 g per litre of blood · 0.8 g per litre of blood · No limit
    Show the answer0.2 g per litre of blood : In practice, zero drinks.
  3. At a junction with no sign or light, who has priority?
    The one coming from the left · The one coming from the right · The fastest · The biggest vehicle
    Show the answerThe one coming from the right : That's "priority to the right".
  4. Is the seat belt compulsory in the back?
    Yes, always · No, never · Only on the motorway · Only at night
    Show the answerYes, always : Compulsory in the back since 1990.
  5. What is the maximum number of points on a driving licence?
    12 · 10 · 15 · 20
    Show the answer12 : The maximum is 12 points.
  6. How many points do you lose for running a red light?
    4 · 1 · 2 · 6
    Show the answer4 : Plus a €135 fine.
  7. What does a yellow diamond sign mean?
    Priority road · Danger · End of road · Roundabout
    Show the answerPriority road : You have priority at junctions.
  8. From what age can you start accompanied driving in France?
    15 · 14 · 16 · 17
    Show the answer15 : With a supervisor who has held a licence for at least 5 years.
  9. What does a flashing amber light mean?
