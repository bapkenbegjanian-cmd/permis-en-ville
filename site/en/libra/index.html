About the sign Libra

Libra is an air sign ruled by Venus, the planet of harmony, beauty and connection. You have a graceful sense of balance and a genuine interest in fairness. Your charm and diplomacy help people find common ground and appreciate life's finer details.

Character traits

  • Diplomatic
  • Charming
  • Fair-minded
  • Gracious

In love

You love with warmth, grace and a sincere wish for harmony. Companionship is important to you, and you enjoy partnerships built on mutual respect and conversation. You show affection through thoughtful attention, shared beauty and a gentle sense of humour.

At work

At work you are valued for your ability to bring people together and see different perspectives. You do well in collaborative settings, negotiation and roles that involve design or aesthetics. Your calm manner helps teams move forward with cooperation and goodwill.

The advice

Trust your own opinion as much as you value others', because your perspective adds balance that the group needs.
