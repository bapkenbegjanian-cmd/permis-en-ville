About the sign Aquarius

Aquarius is an air sign ruled by Uranus, the planet of originality, innovation and fresh perspectives. You think independently and care about ideas that could help people live better together. Your unusual insight and friendly spirit make you a valued companion.

Character traits

  • Original
  • Humanitarian
  • Independent
  • Inventive

In love

You love with openness and a respect for each person's individuality. You enjoy friendship as much as romance and appreciate partners who share your curiosity and sense of freedom. Your affection feels warm and considerate, especially when you can be yourself fully.

At work

At work you bring creative solutions and a forward-looking outlook. You do well in innovative fields, community projects or roles that improve how people work or live. Your independence helps you explore new approaches with confidence.
