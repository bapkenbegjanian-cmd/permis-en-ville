About the sign Gemini

Gemini is an air sign ruled by Mercury, the planet of communication, curiosity and quick thinking. You are naturally talkative, inventive and eager to learn. Your mind moves lightly from one idea to the next, and you often connect people and ideas in surprising ways.

Character traits

  • Curious
  • Witty
  • Adaptable
  • Sociable

In love

You love through conversation, humour and shared discovery. You are at your best with a partner who keeps up with your ideas and gives you room to explore. Affection for you often grows through long talks and playful exchanges that feel fresh every time.

At work

At work you shine in roles that involve communication, learning or variety. You are quick to adapt, good at explaining complex things and happy to juggle several projects at once. Your curiosity keeps you growing, especially when you can share what you discover with others.

The advice

Choose one interest to follow deeply this season, because your curious mind will enjoy the richness that depth brings.
