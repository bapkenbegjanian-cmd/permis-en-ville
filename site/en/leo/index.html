About the sign Leo

Leo is a fire sign ruled by the Sun, the source of light, warmth and vitality. You have a natural presence and a generous heart that inspires others. You enjoy creativity, celebration and the joy of being fully yourself.

Character traits

  • Generous
  • Confident
  • Creative
  • Warm-hearted

In love

You love with passion and sincerity, offering affection that is warm, expressive and fun. You enjoy romance that feels special and celebrated. Partners who appreciate your loyalty and your big-hearted nature help you feel truly seen and cherished.

At work

At work you bring energy, creativity and a natural gift for encouraging others. You do well in roles where you can express your ideas, lead with enthusiasm or represent something you believe in. Your confidence inspires teams to aim high and to take pride in their work.

The advice

Make space to celebrate others as well, because your joy grows even brighter when you share the spotlight.
