About the sign Scorpio

Scorpio is a water sign ruled by Pluto, the planet associated with depth, transformation and renewal. You have intensity, insight and a rich inner life. Your loyalty runs deep, and you are drawn to truths that lie beneath the surface.

Character traits

  • Perceptive
  • Passionate
  • Resilient
  • Private

In love

You love with depth and intensity, offering loyalty and emotional honesty to those you trust. Closeness is precious to you, and you feel most alive in relationships that allow real vulnerability. Partners who respect your privacy and match your sincerity help you flourish.

At work

At work you are known for focus, determination and a talent for seeing what others miss. You handle complex challenges with calm strength and often excel in research, strategy or work that calls for discretion. Your steadiness helps you rebuild and grow even after difficult periods.

The advice

Share a little more of your inner world with someone you trust, because connection can deepen when you let others in.
