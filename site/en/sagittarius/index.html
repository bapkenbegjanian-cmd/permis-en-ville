About the sign Sagittarius

Sagittarius is a fire sign ruled by Jupiter, the planet of expansion, wisdom and adventure. You are optimistic, curious about the wider world and eager to explore new ideas. Your open spirit inspires others to look beyond the familiar.

Character traits

  • Adventurous
  • Optimistic
  • Honest
  • Philosophical

In love

You love with enthusiasm, openness and a playful sense of wonder. You enjoy partners who share your appetite for travel, conversation and discovery. Your affection is generous and sincere, and you grow happiest when your relationship leaves room for freedom and shared growth.

At work

At work you thrive in roles that offer variety, learning and a sense of purpose. You are a natural teacher, storyteller or explorer of new fields. Your big-picture thinking helps teams imagine possibilities that others had not considered.

The advice

Finish one project you have started before beginning the next, since completing your ideas will bring a satisfaction that new beginnings alone cannot give.
