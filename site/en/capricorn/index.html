About the sign Capricorn

Capricorn is an earth sign ruled by Saturn, the planet of structure, discipline and lasting achievement. You are patient, responsible and driven by a clear sense of purpose. Your steady progress builds foundations that others can rely on for years.

Character traits

  • Disciplined
  • Ambitious
  • Reliable
  • Wise

In love

You love with quiet devotion and a commitment to building something lasting together. You show care through dependable actions more than grand declarations. Partners who value sincerity, shared goals and gentle affection help you open up and relax.

At work

At work you are respected for your perseverance, organisation and long-term vision. You enjoy setting goals and steadily working toward them. Leadership roles and projects that require patience and strategy suit your strengths well.

The advice

Make time for play and rest, because your hard work is richer when it is balanced with moments of simple enjoyment.
