About the sign Aries

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, a fire sign ruled by Mars, the planet of courage and drive. You arrive at new beginnings with energy and enthusiasm, and you are often the one who gets things moving. Your warmth is direct and generous, and people tend to feel your sincerity right away.

Character traits

  • Courageous
  • Direct
  • Energetic
  • Independent

In love

You love with open-hearted honesty and a playful spark. You enjoy the thrill of new romance and show affection through bold gestures and easy, spontaneous adventures. Your partners appreciate your loyalty once you have chosen them, and you grow most content when you feel both passion and respect.

At work

At work you thrive on challenges, fresh starts and goals you can see clearly. You are quick to take initiative and comfortable leading a team through change. Your greatest strength is turning an idea into action, especially when you can pace yourself and make room for patience.

The advice

Give yourself a pause before you rush ahead, because a moment of reflection can make your brave choices even stronger.
