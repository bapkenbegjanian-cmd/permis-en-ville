About the sign Taurus

Taurus is an earth sign ruled by Venus, the planet of beauty, comfort and pleasure. You bring steadiness, patience and a deep appreciation for the good things in life. Your calm presence makes others feel at home, and you value what is genuine and lasting.

Character traits

  • Dependable
  • Patient
  • Sensual
  • Grounded

In love

You love with loyalty and tenderness, expressing care through thoughtful gestures, shared meals and quiet routines. Once you feel secure, you offer a devotion that feels warm and reliable. You are happiest with a partner who values sincerity and unhurried moments together.

At work

At work you are known for consistency, careful effort and a good eye for quality. You build things that last and bring calm to busy teams. You do especially well in roles where patience, craftsmanship or a sense of beauty are valued.

The advice

Let yourself try something new now and then, since a small change can bring fresh pleasure to the comfort you have built.
