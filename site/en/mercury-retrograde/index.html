What does Mercury retrograde mean?

Mercury retrograde is an optical illusion: seen from Earth, the planet appears to move backwards in the sky for a few weeks, about three to four times a year. In astrology it is linked to a period of mix-ups: misunderstood messages, travel delays, tech glitches and projects that stall. There is no scientific basis for this belief, but many people use it as a useful reminder to slow down, reread, double-check and revisit what was left aside.

Our tips for getting through it

  • Reread your messages and contracts before sending or signing them.
  • Leave extra time for trips and appointments.
  • Back up your important files before any update.
  • Use the time to finish, review or resume a project rather than start a new one.
