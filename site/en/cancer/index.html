About the sign Cancer

Cancer is a water sign ruled by the Moon, the planet linked with feeling, memory and home. You are caring, intuitive and deeply attentive to the people you love. Your sensitivity is one of your gifts, allowing you to create warmth wherever you go.

Character traits

  • Nurturing
  • Intuitive
  • Loyal
  • Protective

In love

You love with tenderness and devotion, showing care through comfort, attentive listening and a home full of small kindnesses. You need emotional closeness and a sense of safety to open fully. Partners who respect your feelings and share your love of family often bring out your most generous side.

At work

At work you are valued for empathy, reliability and a genuine concern for others. You often create supportive environments and notice what people need before they say it. Roles connected with care, teamwork or creating a welcoming atmosphere suit you well.

The advice

Be gentle with yourself on days when your feelings run high, since your sensitivity is a strength when you give it room to breathe.
