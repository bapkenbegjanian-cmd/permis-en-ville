About the sign Virgo

Virgo is an earth sign ruled by Mercury, the planet of thought, detail and useful communication. You are thoughtful, observant and dedicated to doing things well. Your practical wisdom and quiet care make you a dependable companion and a steady guide for others.

Character traits

  • Thoughtful
  • Meticulous
  • Helpful
  • Observant

In love

You love through practical care, attentive listening and small acts that show you are paying attention. You appreciate honesty and a partner who respects your need for order and personal space. Your devotion is steady, and you grow closest to people who share your appreciation for everyday kindness.

At work

At work you are known for precision, careful planning and a strong sense of responsibility. You enjoy improving systems and making sure details are right. Roles that involve analysis, organisation or service to others allow your talents to shine.

The advice

Allow yourself to enjoy things that are imperfect, because joy often lives in the moments that do not need to be flawless.
