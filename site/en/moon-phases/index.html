The 8 phases of the moon

New moon

The new moon is a time of quiet beginnings and fresh intentions. Many people feel reflective and ready to start something small but meaningful. It is traditionally said to favour planting seeds, setting goals and making a clear wish. Take a gentle moment to decide what you would like to nurture in the weeks ahead.

Waxing crescent

The waxing crescent brings a rising sense of hope and momentum. Energy often returns gradually, and ideas begin to take shape. Tradition associates this phase with growth, new friendships and taking the first practical steps. Let your intentions grow steadily, one small action at a time.

First quarter

The first quarter moon often brings determination and a push toward action. You may feel motivated to overcome small obstacles and make decisions. It is traditionally said to favour courage, planning and commitment. This is a good time to tidy up loose ends and move forward with confidence.

Waxing gibbous

The waxing gibbous moon is a season of refinement and steady effort. Things that began earlier now benefit from patience and attention to detail. Tradition links this phase with polishing work, improving relationships and preparing for fulfilment. Keep your focus on quality and enjoy the progress you are making.

Full moon

The full moon is often described as a time of heightened feeling and clarity. Emotions may run more vividly, and things long hidden can come into view. Tradition favours celebration, honest conversations and gratitude for what has been achieved. Allow yourself to enjoy the light while staying kind to yourself and others.

Waning gibbous

The waning gibbous moon invites gratitude and sharing. You may feel inclined to give back, teach or simply enjoy the company of others. Tradition associates this phase with generosity, reflection on lessons learned and gentle letting go. Notice what has worked well and carry those lessons forward.

Last quarter

The last quarter moon encourages reflection and release. It is a natural time to review your habits, clear away what no longer serves you and make room for change. Tradition favours tidying, forgiveness and honest self-assessment. Take your time, and let this phase help you travel lightly into what comes next.

Waning crescent

The waning crescent is a quiet, restful phase that invites rest and inner reflection. Energy often slows, and intuition may become more prominent. Tradition associates this time with recovery, dreaming and preparing for a fresh cycle. Give yourself permission to slow down, listen to your heart and gather strength for new beginnings.
