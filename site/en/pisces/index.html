About the sign Pisces

Pisces is a water sign ruled by Neptune, the planet of imagination, compassion and dreams. You are gentle, intuitive and deeply moved by beauty and feeling. Your empathy allows you to understand others in a special way.

  • Imaginative
  • Gentle
  • Intuitive

In love

You love with tenderness, empathy and a romantic heart. You show affection through kindness, creativity and a willingness to understand your partner's inner world. You feel most secure with someone who is honest, supportive and appreciates your sensitive nature.

At work
